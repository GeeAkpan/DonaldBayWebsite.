The Engineering Brief

The Atlantic Coastal Corridor required navigating complex mangrove terrain, tidal water table pressures, and high-tonnage freight demands. Donalds Bay Limited deployed proprietary deep-soil stabilization, heavy-duty polymer asphalt formulations, and precast concrete bridge spans. The project opened 90 days ahead of schedule, facilitating over 45,000 daily vehicular transits with zero safety incidents.
